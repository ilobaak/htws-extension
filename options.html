Hibernate Tabs, Windows, and Sessions

Settings

Choose when inactive tabs hibernate, what must stay open, and how deeply the extension may inspect page activity. Nothing is sent to us. Chrome may sync scalar settings through your browser profile.

What the Extension Reads

Tab URLs, titles, and activity state are evaluated inside your browser to apply exclusions and decide when a tab is safe to hibernate. Optional website protection adds only yes-or-no signals for playing media and modified fields. It never reads form values or page text.
